Privacy policy · effective with Tacto 1.3

Privacy

Tacto does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

Anonymous usage stats

From version 1.3, Tacto can send anonymous usage counts, for example that the metronome was started at a tempo between 80 and 100 BPM, that the tuner was opened, or that the Pro screen was shown and from where. These counts use a random identifier created on your iPhone that is not linked to you, your Apple Account or any other app. They never include audio, names, contact details or location. They are processed by TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com), which hashes the identifier again before storing it, and are used only to decide what to improve. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats; turning it off also deletes anything not yet sent. Nothing is sent from the China mainland App Store.

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone only while the Tuner screen is open, to measure the pitch of the note you play. Audio is analysed in memory on your iPhone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your settings

Tempos, setlists and practice minutes are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Tacto Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Tacto only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Tacto is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
