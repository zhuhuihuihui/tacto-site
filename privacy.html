Privacy policy · effective with Tacto 1.7

Privacy

Tacto has no account and does not sell personal data. From version 1.7 the free version shows one banner ad, served by Google AdMob, and uses Google Firebase for usage stats and remote settings. Tacto Pro removes the ad. This page lists what each one receives.

Ads (free version only)

The banner is served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google receives your device's IP address, device model and OS version, and ad interactions (views and taps). From the second launch, iOS asks whether Tacto may let Google track you across other companies' apps and websites. Only if you allow it does Google also receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalise ads; if you don't, ads are not personalised. In regions that require it, Google's consent form asks first, and you can change that choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Ads are limited to general-audience content. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. No ads are shown to Tacto Pro users or in the China mainland App Store, and nothing ad-related runs for them.

Usage stats

Tacto can send usage events, for example that the metronome was started at a tempo between 80 and 100 BPM, that the tuner was opened, or that the Pro screen was shown and from where. These counts use a random identifier created on your iPhone that is not linked to you, your Apple Account or any other app. They never include audio, names, contact details or location. They are processed by TelemetryDeck (telemetrydeck.com), which hashes the identifier again before storing it, and, from version 1.7, by Google Firebase Analytics, which also receives a Firebase app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version and coarse location derived from the IP address. They are used only to decide what to improve, never for ads. You can turn them off at any time in Settings → Share usage stats; turning it off also deletes anything not yet sent. Nothing is sent from the China mainland App Store.

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone only while the Tuner screen is open, to measure the pitch of the note you play. Audio is analysed in memory on your iPhone and is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your settings

Tempos, setlists and practice minutes are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Tacto Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Tacto only learns whether Pro is active on the device.

Children

Tacto is rated 4+. It is not directed at children, ads are limited to general-audience content, and it asks for no names, contact details or location.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
